## Scoring guidance

**Impact**
- High — threatens committed outcome, milestone, regulatory obligation or executive decision.
- Medium — material disruption but manageable within programme tolerance.
- Low — limited impact; workstream can resolve locally.

## Escalation rule
Escalate when a risk or issue moves outside agreed tolerance, lacks an owner, blocks another workstream, or requires a sponsor-level decision.
