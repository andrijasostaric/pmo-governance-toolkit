# Governance Cadence

| Forum | Frequency | Purpose | Core attendees | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workstream delivery check | Weekly | Review milestones, actions and blockers | Programme Manager + Workstream Leads | Updated actions and RAID |
| Cross-workstream dependency review | Weekly | Resolve sequencing and ownership dependencies | Programme Manager + relevant leads | Dependency decisions / escalations |
| Steering Committee | Bi-weekly | Review status, major risks and decisions required | Sponsor + Programme Manager + senior leads | Decisions, risk acceptance, direction |
| Readiness review | Milestone-based | Confirm operational, technical and control readiness | Delivery, Operations, Compliance, Engineering | Go / no-go recommendation |
| Retrospective | End of phase | Capture lessons and improve delivery model | Core programme team | Agreed improvements |

## Meeting design
- Circulate pre-read before governance forums.
- Use live meetings for decisions and exceptions, not status reading.
- Record decisions, owners and due dates immediately.
- Escalate unresolved items according to impact and tolerance.
- Keep governance proportionate to programme complexity.
