# Project Charter — Transformation Programme

## Purpose
Create a common operating model across five business and technology workstreams, reducing duplicated processes and improving visibility of ownership, risks and delivery status.

## Objectives
- Establish a single programme plan and governance model.
- Clarify accountable owners for deliverables and decisions.
- Standardise risk, dependency and issue management.
- Improve executive visibility through concise weekly reporting.
- Deliver agreed priority changes without disrupting business-as-usual operations.

## Scope
### In scope
- Process discovery and current-state mapping
- Target operating model definition
- Workstream planning and milestone tracking
- RACI and ownership definition
- RAID management
- Governance and reporting
- Change/readiness coordination

### Out of scope
- Replacing core business applications
- Large-scale organisational restructuring
- Changes not approved through programme governance

## Success measures
- 100% of priority deliverables have a named accountable owner.
- Material risks and dependencies are reviewed weekly.
- Decisions are logged with owner and due date.
- Steering updates focus on outcomes, blockers and decisions required.
- No critical milestone is missed without prior escalation.

## Governance
**Executive Sponsor:** COO  
**Programme Lead:** Project / Programme Manager  
**Workstream Leads:** Product, Engineering, Operations, Compliance, Finance

## Delivery approach
Hybrid delivery model using milestone-based planning for programme-level control and Agile/Kanban practices within individual workstreams where appropriate.
